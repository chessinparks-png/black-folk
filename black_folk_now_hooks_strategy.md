# BLACK FOLK — NOW Hooks Strategy

## Purpose
Give BLACK FOLK more present-day energy without making the app bigger and without rewriting, shortening, or reordering the historical curriculum.

NOW hooks are a refreshable contemporary layer. They are never the lesson itself. They either open a historical idea with a vivid present-day echo or close the idea by showing that the underlying question still exists.

## Core rule
A NOW hook must be removable without damaging the historical lesson.

## Placement triage
Before writing hooks, classify every eligible idea, thread, and debate as one of:

### LEAD
Use when a strong, specific, verified modern echo improves curiosity before the history appears.

### CODA
Use when a modern echo is useful mainly as proof that the old question persists, or when leading with it would spoil the historical idea.

### NONE
Use when no honest modern echo exists, the connection would be merely thematic, or the hook would cheapen or oversimplify the history.

Do not force LEAD everywhere. Expect a real mix.

## Research window
Prefer examples from 2018 to the present.
Use 2015–2017 only when the example is unusually strong.

## What counts as a real echo
The modern example must share the same underlying:
- question
- mechanism
- institutional structure
- strategic tension
- tradeoff

Sharing only a topic is not enough.

## Hook structure
For each approved LEAD or CODA item:

- `text`: 1–2 sentences about a real event, statistic, law, cultural moment, business development, technology change, or debate.
- `bridge`: one sentence connecting the modern example to the specific historical material already taught.
- `source`: a reliable source actually opened and checked.
- `refreshBy`: `YYYY-MM`
- `placement`: `LEAD` or `CODA`

Every bridge must be unique to its historical idea.

Do not reuse stock openings such as:
- “This argument is older than you think.”
- “The same debate continues today.”
- “History repeats itself.”

## Source hierarchy
Prefer:
1. government, courts, universities, original data, research institutions
2. major reputable news organizations
3. reputable trade, cultural, or professional publications
4. advocacy organizations only when the claim is about that organization itself

If a claim cannot be verified, leave it out.

Use “reportedly” when something is reported but not independently documented.

## Accuracy and complication
Accuracy over flair.

If a modern effort:
- stalled
- failed
- changed direction
- drew substantial criticism
- produced mixed results

say so.

Do not turn NOW hooks into inspirational success stories.

## Political neutrality
BLACK FOLK presents real disagreements without choosing a political winner.

Across the full set, include a range of documented Black political perspectives when genuinely relevant, including conservative, centrist, progressive, nationalist, liberal, socialist, abolitionist, religious, entrepreneurial, and other traditions.

Do not manufacture balance inside a single hook.

Do not make evaluative judgments about current political candidates, parties, elections, or ballot choices.

## Arena diversity
Vary examples across:
- music
- film
- visual culture
- sports
- technology
- law
- business
- education
- local news
- housing
- labor
- health systems
- media
- global Black communities

Avoid making the layer mostly celebrities or mostly policing/courts.

## Relationship to historical content
Do not:
- rewrite existing historical content
- shorten existing historical content
- reorder existing historical content
- replace historical explanations with modern examples

The modern layer must connect back to the specific person, place, year, institution, movement, or event already taught.

## Relationship to BLACK FOLK's hidden lens
NOW hooks should naturally surface recurring questions such as:

- WHO HAS POWER?
- WHO OWNS IT?
- WHO DECIDES?
- WHO IS “WE”?
- DOES REPRESENTATION EQUAL CONTROL?
- DOES THIS HELP ONE PERSON OR CHANGE THE SYSTEM?
- WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE PROTEST?
- REFORM IT, LEAVE IT, OR BUILD SOMETHING ELSE?
- WHO DESIGNED THIS — AND WHO COULD REDESIGN IT?

But no hook should force a predetermined answer.

## Workflow

### STEP 1 — TRIAGE
Sort every idea, thread, and debate into:
- LEAD
- CODA
- NONE

Output:
`item · world · placement · one-line reason`

Stop for approval before drafting hooks.

### STEP 2 — DRAFT
After approval, work one world at a time.

Output after each world:
`item · placement · text · bridge · source · refreshBy`

Also include a short list of:
- uncertain examples
- sourcing limitations
- items intentionally left without hooks

Wait for approval before moving to the next world.

### STEP 3 — COMPILE
At the end, compile all approved hooks into a clean developer-ready list or JSON file.

## Product guardrail
The goal is not to make BLACK FOLK “newsier.”

The goal is to make the player feel:

**This idea is still alive.**

Then send them back into the history.
