# WHAT DOES FREEDOM REQUIRE?

## 1. DISCOVER · +10 KNOWLEDGE

**RECONSTRUCTION**

Ending slavery answered one question. Freedom created several more.

*Tap to reveal*

Citizenship, voting, education, land, labor, and political power still had to be fought over.

---

## 2. WHY THEN · +15 KNOWLEDGE

Why does Reconstruction matter to the meaning of freedom?

- It shows that ending slavery did not settle questions of citizenship and power.
- It was the beginning of the Atlantic slave trade.
- It created the Harlem Renaissance.
- It ended all legal segregation immediately.

**REVEAL:** Emancipation answered whether slavery would continue. Reconstruction raised the harder question of what freedom would require next.

---

## 3. SAME QUESTION · +25 KNOWLEDGE

Baker and King emphasized different strengths in leadership. Which contrast is fairest?

- grassroots leadership development vs. visible centralized leadership
- education vs. no education
- violence vs. no politics
- culture vs. economics

**REVEAL:** Baker stressed building leadership from below. King’s public leadership could concentrate attention, fundraising, and national pressure. The tradeoff matters more than picking a winner.

---

## 4. WHAT · +10 KNOWLEDGE

Booker T. Washington’s strategy emphasized…

- industrial education and economic strength
- armed insurrection
- decentralized student leadership
- literary modernism

**REVEAL:** Washington emphasized education, work, and economic advancement in a hostile political environment.

---

## 5. RECALL · +20 KNOWLEDGE

Think before revealing:

What is the core difference between individual mobility and collective freedom?

**REVEAL:** An individual can advance while the rules or conditions facing the wider group remain largely unchanged.

SELF-RATE: KNEW IT / ALMOST / MISSED

Put these in chronological order.

**REVEAL:** 1820s → 1860s–70s → 1920s → 1960s.
