# Respectability revision (after V1.5)

The V1.5 Respectability material read as promotional ("real advantages", "strategically useful",
"present respectability's strongest case"). It was rewritten in place, in the V1.5 source files,
to follow the supporting source framing more closely:

- **Black Perspectives audit**: vengeance feminism as a challenge to respectability, respectability
  as an impossible standard for poor, criminalized, unlettered, queer, or otherwise
  "non-respectable" Black people, disrepute as a possible price of self-defense where the law
  failed, who counts as an acceptable victim or political subject, and who counts as a thinker.
- **Keywords for African American Studies**: its critique of morality and respectability
  politics that exclude queer and trans people, sex workers, nontraditional families, and people
  treated as deviant.
