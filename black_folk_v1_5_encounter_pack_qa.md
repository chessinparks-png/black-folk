## Recommended early V1.5 bridge sessions

These are **not** five more mandatory onboarding screens. They are curated theme bundles the scheduler can surface early after the first three sessions, with review cards mixed between them.

### S04 — WHO GETS CALLED DANGEROUS?
**Flow:** D049 → E068 → D045 → E058 → E057 → E076
**KEEP THIS:** Before punishment or protection, ask who was defined as dangerous—or worthy.
**Purpose:** Introduce Criminalization and Respectability together without making refusal the automatic correct answer.

### S05 — ACCESS OR CONTROL?
**Flow:** D050 → E071 → D047 → E063 → E072 → E064
**KEEP THIS:** Getting in and having control are different questions.
**Purpose:** Introduce Predatory Inclusion and Black Nationalism through access, terms, ownership, and decision-making.

### S06 — BLACKNESS ACROSS BORDERS
**Flow:** D042 → D048 → E053 → E065 → E066 → E074
**KEEP THIS:** Connection across borders is real without becoming sameness across borders.
**Purpose:** Introduce Black immigrant identity, Diaspora as a concept, and Pan-Africanism as an organizing project.

### S07 — WHO IS “WE”?
**Flow:** D017 → E048 → D039 → E023 → E076 → E064
**KEEP THIS:** When politics says “we,” ask whose life is being treated as the norm.
**Purpose:** Turn WHO IS “WE”? into a recurring reflex through Stewart, Combahee, and collective politics.

### S08 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE END?
**Flow:** D046 → E059 → D011 → E061 → E060 → E077
**KEEP THIS:** Ending a system and building freedom are different jobs.
**Purpose:** Introduce Abolition inside LEAVE · REFORM · BUILD without making it the app’s predetermined political answer.
